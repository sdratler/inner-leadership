## WHAT THE TREATMENT IS

Heading:

Individual therapy. Real group situations. Parent guidance. One focused process.

Body:

Each boy meets individually once a week to develop language, judgment and a specific therapeutic target. Twice a week, he enters structured project situations in which communication, frustration, responsibility, disagreement and help-seeking become visible. The next individual session uses what actually happened as therapeutic material. Three parent-guidance sessions help the same language carry into the home.

## WHY THE LABS MATTER

Heading:

A child may understand the skill and still lose access to it under pressure.

Body:

A conversation can show what a boy understands. A shared project shows what happens when another boy rejects his idea, a plan fails, a tool must be shared or the group needs a decision. The project is not an enrichment extra. It creates the real conditions in which therapeutic work can be observed, practiced and retried.

Method line:

Understand → Practice → Observe → Reflect → Retry

## WHO IT IS FOR

Heading:

For boys who need practical work on communication, regulation, judgment and responsibility.

Body:

The process is for boys ages 7–13, subject to fit, who remain in their current school or educational framework and can participate safely in individual treatment and a small applied group. Parents must be able to bring their son to Beit Shemesh for the weekly structure and participate in three parent-guidance sessions.

Possible parent goals may include:

- handling disagreement or confrontation more effectively;
- finding words instead of shutting down, exploding or avoiding;
- staying engaged when a task becomes difficult;
- thinking through options and consequences;
- navigating peer situations with better judgment;
- taking more ownership without constant adult management;
- building confidence from real competence.

This page does not diagnose a child. Final fit is decided through the qualification and parent conversation.

## WHAT EVERY BOY RECEIVES

### 12 Individual Therapeutic Sessions

One 50–60-minute session each week focused on language, emotional regulation, judgment, goals, reflection and preparation for real situations.

### 24 Applied Therapeutic Project Labs

Two 90-minute small-group labs each week using woodworking, building, cooking, movement and collaborative challenges to create observable therapeutic material.

### Three Parent-Guidance Sessions

Focused work on motivation and agency, communication and critical thinking, and the gradual transfer of responsibility at home.

## FOUR TREATMENT AREAS

### 1. Values, Identity, Purpose & Goals

Clarify what matters, connect values to choices, and turn direction into one concrete responsibility.

### 2. Communication

State what he wants, listen accurately, disagree without attacking, set boundaries, negotiate and repair.

### 3. Problem Solving

Separate facts from assumptions, generate options, evaluate consequences, choose, test and revise.

### 4. Bodily Awareness

Notice hunger, fatigue, tension, overstimulation, movement and recovery as information that affects judgment and communication.

## PARENT GUIDANCE

Heading:

The child is not working in a separate system from the home.

Body:

Parents receive three concentrated sessions:

1. Motivation, Agency, Values & Purpose.
2. Communication, Relationship & Critical Thinking.

The goal is not to remove boundaries. It is to stop doing unnecessary thinking and management for a child who is ready to learn more ownership.
