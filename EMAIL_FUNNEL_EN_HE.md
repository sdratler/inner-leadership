## E02 — Internal Motivation
Trigger: Day 2 if not enrolled
### English
Subject: Getting the behavior is not the same as building motivation
Rewards, pressure and constant supervision can produce behavior. That does not automatically mean the child has internalized the reason for doing it.
The more useful question is: what would he need to understand, value and manage if I were not standing over him?
That shift—from external management toward internal judgment—is the core of self-governance.
Watch or continue the masterclass: [MASTERCLASS LINK]
### עברית

## E03 — Critical Thinking
Trigger: Day 4 if not enrolled
### English
Subject: Make him do more of the thinking
When a child brings a problem, adults often solve it quickly.
Before giving the answer, try:
What actually happened?
What is the real problem?
What options do you have?
What is likely to happen with each option?
What will you try?
This is not about withholding help. It is about giving the child the part of the thinking he is developmentally capable of doing.
[MASTERCLASS LINK]

## E04 — Communication
Trigger: Day 6 if not enrolled
### English
Subject: Communication starts with the result you want
A useful communication question is not only “How do I feel?”
It is also: what do I want this person to understand, and what result am I trying to create?
Children need words for requests, disagreement, boundaries, uncertainty and repair. They also need enough regulation to use those words when the conversation is difficult.
That is why communication is one of the four core treatment areas in the 12-week process.

## E05 — Applied Projects
Trigger: Day 8 if not enrolled
### English
Subject: Why the projects matter
A conversation can tell us what a child understands.
A shared project shows what he does when somebody rejects his idea, a measurement is wrong, a tool is shared, the plan fails, or the group needs a decision.
That is the reason for the therapeutic project labs. The project creates real therapeutic and developmental material.
The individual therapeutic session gives language and direction. The lab gives reality. The next individual session turns that experience into therapeutic work.
[PROGRAM LINK]

## E06 — Bodily Awareness
Trigger: Day 10 if not enrolled
### English
Subject: The body changes the quality of a decision
Hunger, fatigue, tension, overstimulation, sleep and movement affect the quality of a child's judgment and communication.
Bodily awareness is not a separate wellness add-on. It is information.
A child who notices what is happening earlier has more opportunity to choose what to do next.
The final module combines body awareness, food, movement, strength, recovery and practical self-care.
[PROGRAM LINK]
