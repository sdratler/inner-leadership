## E02 — Internal Motivation

Trigger: Day 2 if not booked, not enrolled and still opted in

### English

Subject: Getting the behavior is not the same as building motivation

Pressure, rewards and constant supervision can produce behavior. That does not automatically mean a child has internalized the reason or learned to manage himself.

A more useful question is:

What would he need to understand, value and manage if I were not standing over him?

That movement from external management toward internal judgment is one of the central aims of Inner Leadership.

## E03 — Critical Thinking

Trigger: Day 4 if not booked, not enrolled and still opted in

### English

Subject: Let him do more of the thinking

When a child brings a problem, adults often solve it quickly.

Before giving the answer, try asking:

What actually happened?
What is the real problem?
What do you know for sure?
What are you assuming?
What options do you have?
What is likely to happen with each option?
What will you try?

This is not withholding help. It is giving the child the part of the thinking he is developmentally capable of doing.

## E04 — Communication

Trigger: Day 6 if not booked, not enrolled and still opted in

### English

Subject: Communication starts with the result you want

A useful communication question is not only “How do I feel?”

It is also:

What do I need the other person to understand?
What do I want to happen next?
What words make that result more likely?

Children need language for requests, disagreement, boundaries, uncertainty and repair. They also need enough regulation to use those words when the conversation becomes difficult.

That is one of the four central treatment areas in the 12-week process.

## E05 — Applied Therapeutic Labs

Trigger: Day 8 if not booked, not enrolled and still opted in

### English

Subject: Why the applied labs matter

A conversation can tell us what a child understands.

A shared project shows what he does when somebody rejects his idea, a measurement is wrong, a tool must be shared, the plan fails or the group needs a decision.

That is why Inner Leadership combines individual treatment with applied therapeutic labs.

The individual session develops language and direction. The lab creates reality. The next individual session turns what happened into therapeutic work.

## E06 — Bodily Awareness

Trigger: Day 10 if not booked, not enrolled and still opted in

### English

Subject: The body changes the quality of a decision

Hunger, fatigue, tension, overstimulation, sleep and movement affect the quality of a child’s judgment and communication.

Bodily awareness is not a separate wellness add-on. It is information.

A boy who notices what is happening earlier has more opportunity to choose what to do next rather than being managed automatically by the state of his body.

The fourth treatment area combines body awareness, food, movement, strength, recovery and practical self-care.
