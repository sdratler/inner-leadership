### English category

**Practical Emotional Therapy for Boys Ages 7–13**

### English delivery descriptor

**A focused 12-week therapeutic process combining one weekly individual therapeutic session, two 90-minute applied project-based group sessions each week, and three parent-guidance sessions.**

The treatment category leads. The 12-week curriculum explains the structure and method. Do not present the offer as a course, chug, club, enrichment program, or generic life-skills class.

## 2. Founder positioning

Preferred introduction:

English:

**I’m Shloimie Dratler. I specialize in intrinsic motivation, emotional regulation, and self-governance in boys ages 7–13.**

Do not invent a licensed title that the owner did not state. Do not hide the service behind vague “coaching” language when describing the Hebrew offer.

## 3. Core message

The treatment is for boys who need more than talking alone. Individual sessions build language and direction. Applied therapeutic project labs create real interpersonal and practical situations. The next individual session uses what actually happened as therapeutic material. Parent guidance changes the home environment around the same targets.

Core loop:

**להבין → לתרגל → לצפות → לשקף → לנסות שוב**

## 4. Visual hierarchy

1. **Trust creative:** real founder photograph or real founder-led video.
2. **Therapeutic-method creative:** real or editorial scene showing a serious interaction, reflection, confrontation, or guided conversation.
3. **Proof creative:** soldering, woodworking, building, cooking, movement, and other real project work.

The soldering image is credible proof of the method but must not lead the therapy-positioned campaign by itself. It reads as activity/enrichment unless the therapeutic frame is already established.

Never generate a fake version of the founder's body, office, or therapy interaction. Use the real founder identity.

## 8. Masterclass promise

The masterclass must be substantive and source-based. It should show parents:

1. the difference between obtaining compliance and building internal motivation;
2. autonomy, competence, and relatedness as the foundation of internalization;
3. how adult over-management weakens agency;
4. questions that make the child define the problem and consider options;
5. communication aimed at a real result;
6. SODAS and natural consequences;
7. how conflict and failed projects become therapeutic material;
8. how body state affects judgment and communication;
9. concrete changes parents can begin implementing at home.

The CTA does not promise a cure. It promises a serious framework, real sources, and practical tools.

## 9. Paid-ad guardrail

For paid Meta creative, do not use direct lines such as:

- “Has your son been diagnosed?”
- “Is the school forcing your son to take medication?”
- “Does your son have ADHD/anxiety?”

Those hooks assert or imply sensitive health information about the viewer's family. Keep the sharper diagnosis/medication discussion inside organic content or the masterclass, framed responsibly and without advising parents to stop or change medication.

The paid ad should describe common situations in general terms and focus on the service, method, and benefits.
