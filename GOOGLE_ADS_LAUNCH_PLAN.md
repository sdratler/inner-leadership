# Inner Leadership — Google Search Launch Plan

Status: **CURRENT / FIRST PAID ACQUISITION CHANNEL**  
Date: 2026-08-23

## Objective

Generate qualified parent conversations and enroll the first two compatible boys without waiting for a long-form masterclass.

## Conversion path

**Hebrew Google Search ad → Hebrew treatment landing page → direct practical qualification → qualified parent booking → consultation → offer → payment**

The exact 10,800 NIS investment and 3 × 3,600 NIS payment structure must be visible before form submission.

## Campaign

Name:

`IL | Google Search | HE | Direct Qualification | 2026-08`

Initial settings:

- Search Network only;
- Hebrew first;
- no Display expansion;
- no Performance Max;
- no Smart Campaign;
- phrase and exact match at launch;
- location option: Presence — people in or regularly in the included locations;
- one campaign rather than one campaign per town;
- one direct landing page;
- no masterclass registration or video-engagement conversion;
- daily budget: 150 NIS;
- fourteen-day validation ceiling: 2,100 NIS;
- do not publish or spend until conversion tracking and the full lead path pass QA.

- תהליך ממוקד בן 12 שבועות
- לבנים בגילאי 7–13
- טיפול אישי ועבודה בקבוצה
- שתי מעבדות טיפוליות בשבוע
- הדרכת הורים כחלק מהתהליך
- פיתוח ויסות ואחריות
- תקשורת ומוטיבציה פנימית
- בבית שמש
- בדיקת התאמה קצרה
- 10,800 ₪ | 3 תשלומים

### Descriptions

1. תהליך טיפולי ממוקד לבנים: פגישה אישית שבועית, שתי מעבדות טיפוליות־מעשיות ושלוש פגישות הדרכת הורים.
2. עובדים על מוטיבציה פנימית, ויסות רגשי, תקשורת, שיקול דעת ואחריות דרך מצבים אמיתיים ולא רק שיחה.
3. התהליך מתקיים בבית שמש במשך 12 שבועות. מחיר מחזור המייסדים: 10,800 ₪ בשלושה תשלומים.
4. קראו את פרטי התהליך ובדקו התאמה מעשית לפני קביעת שיחת הורים.

Do not make diagnosis claims, cure claims or guarantees.

## Landing-page requirements

The Google landing page must answer, above or near the first CTA:

- what the treatment is;
- who it is for;
- ages 7–13;
- Beit Shemesh;
- one weekly individual session;
- two weekly 90-minute therapeutic project labs;
- three parent-guidance sessions;
- 12 weeks;
- 10,800 NIS / 3 × 3,600 NIS;
- direct fit-check CTA.

The optional 5–6-minute program overview may be embedded on the page. It must never block the form.

## Qualification and routing

The direct form collects:

- parent name, email and phone;
- preferred language;
- son age;
- city;
- current educational framework;
- primary goal;
- relevant group/social context;
- interest in practical project work;
- ability to travel to Beit Shemesh;
- maximum travel time;
- weekly schedule fit;
- parent-session commitment;
- financial fit with 10,800 NIS / 3 × 3,600 NIS;
- hidden source detail / UTM / GCLID where supported.

Clear fit proceeds to booking. Uncertain fit goes to Human Review. Do not auto-reject a child from limited public-form data unless an explicit hard rule is met.

## Conversion tracking

### Primary conversion

- completed direct qualification.

### Secondary conversions

- qualified consultation booked;
- consultation attended;
- first payment received;
- call/email click only as diagnostic events, not the primary optimization goal.

Preserve:

- GCLID;
- UTM source, medium, campaign, content and term;
- landing language;
- `IL Source Detail`;
- `IL Ad Market`.

Do not send diagnosis, free-text clinical history, religion or other sensitive form content to advertising platforms.

## Operating rhythm

### Before launch

1. Complete and QA the Hebrew landing, qualification and thank-you route.
2. Show price before form submission.
3. Confirm the qualification form creates/updates one contact and one active IL opportunity.
4. Confirm qualified parents can book the canonical calendar.
5. Confirm immediate internal notification/task creation.
6. Install and test Google Ads conversion tracking.
7. Confirm privacy/terms and consent language.
8. Keep the campaign draft until explicit owner approval to spend.

### First 72 hours

- inspect search terms at least daily;
- add negatives for irrelevant intent;
- verify actual locations;
- verify forms and GCLID/UTM capture;
- respond to qualified submissions as quickly as operationally possible;
- do not make broad creative, landing and bidding changes simultaneously.

### Decision metrics

Track:

- spend;
- impressions;
- search impression share when available;
- clicks;
- CTR;
- CPC;
- qualification starts;
- qualification completions;
- qualified / human-review / not-fit outcomes;
- booked calls;
- attended calls;
- offers;
- first payments;
- revenue;
- cost per attended qualified call;
- cost per enrolled boy.

CTR and cost per click are diagnostic. Enrollment economics decide whether the channel works.
