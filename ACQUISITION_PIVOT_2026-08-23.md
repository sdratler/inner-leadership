# Inner Leadership — Acquisition Pivot

Date: 2026-08-23  
Status: **CURRENT / LOCKED**  
Owner decision: Shloimie Dratler

## Decision

The 35–40-minute parent masterclass is no longer part of the active launch funnel and is not a launch dependency.

The active acquisition system is now:

**Google Search → price-transparent landing page → direct practical qualification → parent consultation → manual offer → first payment → enrollment**

The long Hebrew and English masterclass scripts and related creative are preserved as **DEFERRED / HISTORY**. They may later be rebuilt as nurture or retargeting assets after real parent conversations show that a long educational asset is commercially necessary.

## Active video assets

### 1. Direct founder video

- Target: approximately 30–45 seconds.
- Primary use: Meta, organic social, Facebook Page and landing-page variants.
- Function: identify the problem, introduce Shloimie, state the therapeutic structure, and send the parent directly to the fit check.
- It does not send the parent to a masterclass.

### 2. Program overview video

- Target: approximately 5–6 minutes.
- Primary use: optional embedded video on the landing page.
- Function: explain who the treatment is for, the private/group/parent structure, the method, the four modules, the price, and the next step.
- Watching is optional. No engagement threshold or video gate is used.

## Commercial priority

The immediate objective is to generate qualified parent conversations and enroll the first two compatible paid boys as quickly as possible.

### Channel order

1. Google Search — first paid channel.
2. Local referral and organic distribution — active in parallel.
3. Meta direct-response advertising — second paid test after the direct funnel works end to end.
4. Long-form masterclass — deferred until justified by real conversion evidence.

## Price transparency

The landing page must display the full investment **before** the parent submits the qualification form:

- 10,800 NIS total;
- three installments of 3,600 NIS;
- one 12-week engagement, subject to final enrollment terms.

The primary performance measures are not CTR or cheap leads. They are:

- completed practical qualifications;
- qualified parent conversations booked;
- qualified parent conversations attended;
- offers made;
- first payments received;
- cost per enrolled boy.

Label them DEFERRED or HISTORY and remove them only from the active route, launch checklist and publication gate.
